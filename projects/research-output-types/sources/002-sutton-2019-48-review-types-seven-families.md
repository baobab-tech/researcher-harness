# 48 Review Types in Seven Families: Traditional, Systematic, Review of Reviews, Rapid, Qualitative, Mixed Methods, Purpose-Specific

**Topic:** Review typologies; grouping review types by shared purpose and search method
**Source:** Anthea Sutton, Mark Clowes, Louise Preston, Andrew Booth (ScHARR, University of Sheffield), 2019
**Type:** peer-reviewed
**URL:** https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/151987/
**DOI:** 10.1111/hir.12276
**Published:** 2019-09
**Status:** expert classification

## Finding

Sutton et al. compile 48 review types from 15 published typologies and group them into seven
families by common purpose and characteristics: traditional reviews, systematic reviews, review of
reviews, rapid reviews, qualitative reviews, mixed method reviews, and purpose-specific reviews.
They exclude labels that name a product and not a method, citing "evidence brief", "rapid
response", and "policy brief". They find several pairs used interchangeably with no methodological
difference: overview against umbrella review, narrative synthesis against narrative review, and
scoping against mapping review.

Defining features by family, as the authors state them:

| Family | Defining feature |
|--------|------------------|
| Traditional | purposive sampling; critical, state-of-the-art, narrative, integrative reviews |
| Systematic | comprehensive search; includes meta-analysis and design-specific reviews |
| Review of reviews | includes only systematic reviews or syntheses |
| Rapid | negotiated scope and methods with a client, with shortcuts declared |
| Qualitative | aggregative or interpretive; may search for theory |
| Mixed methods | integrates quantitative and qualitative data; includes realist reviews |
| Purpose-specific | tailored to one purpose: HTA, concept analysis, scoping, mapping |

## Methodology

Review types were compiled from published typologies and the authors' experience, screened against
the inclusion criteria, grouped into families by consensus, and matched to information-retrieval
guidance from review-producing organisations, PubMed, MEDLINE, and Google Scholar.

## Limitations and conflicts

Read from the accepted manuscript on White Rose; the deposit omits Tables 3 and 4, which hold the
full list of 48 types and their definitions, so individual types beyond those named in the text are
not recorded here. Health-focused. Booth co-authored the 2009 typology. No funding stated in the
manuscript.
