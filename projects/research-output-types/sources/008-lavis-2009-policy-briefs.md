# A Policy Brief Packages Systematic Reviews and Local Evidence into Problem, Options, and Implementation for a Decision-Maker

**Topic:** Policy briefs as a decision-support output
**Source:** John Lavis, Govin Permanand, Andrew Oxman, Simon Lewin, Atle Fretheim (SUPPORT collaboration), 2009
**Type:** peer-reviewed (methods guidance)
**URL:** https://health-policy-systems.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/1478-4505-7-S1-S13
**DOI:** 10.1186/1478-4505-7-S1-S13
**Published:** 2009-12
**Status:** methodological guidance

## Finding

Lavis et al. describe the policy brief as a product that "mobilises the best available research
evidence on high-priority issues," drawing mainly on systematic reviews plus local evidence. A brief
describes the problem, what is known and not known about the costs and consequences of options for
addressing it, and key implementation considerations. Briefs are context-specific and make
trade-offs between options explicit.

## Methodology

Guidance article in the SUPPORT Tools series, framed as questions for preparing or assessing a brief.

## Limitations and conflicts

Health policy focus, low- and middle-income country framing. Authors lead the SUPPORT collaboration
and associated knowledge-translation units.
