# Research task and output types: briefing

**Retrieved:** 2026-09-19 · 9 sources · [Index](../_index.md)

The categories below are this project's synthesis. The typologies they draw on come from health
and library science. Each named type links to the source that defines it.

## 1. Process-support tasks

These steps recur inside every review type. They produce inputs to other work, not published
outputs. Garritty et al. list rapid-review recommendations stage by stage, and Arksey and O'Malley
give the scoping stages ([006](../sources/006-garritty-2021-cochrane-rapid-review-methods.md),
[005](../sources/005-arksey-omalley-2005-scoping-framework.md)).

| Task | Output |
|------|--------|
| Question formulation | a structured question; framework varies by question type: PICO, CoCoPop, PIRD ([003](../sources/003-munn-2018-ten-systematic-review-types.md)) |
| Feasibility scan | whether literature exists and whether a review already covers it ([005](../sources/005-arksey-omalley-2005-scoping-framework.md), purpose 2) |
| Search strategy and protocol | databases, query strings, criteria, written down before searching |
| Screening | include/exclude decisions with reasons; dual screening recommended ([006](../sources/006-garritty-2021-cochrane-rapid-review-methods.md)) |
| Extraction or charting | one record per source |
| Appraisal | risk of bias per study; certainty of evidence by GRADE ([006](../sources/006-garritty-2021-cochrane-rapid-review-methods.md)) |
| Citation chasing | references and citations of included studies ([002](../sources/002-sutton-2019-48-review-types-seven-families.md)) |
| Single-source appraisal | critical review of one paper or report |
| Background briefing | terms, actors, data sources, disputes on an unfamiliar topic |

## 2. Evidence syntheses (reviews)

Sutton et al. group 48 review types into seven families
([002](../sources/002-sutton-2019-48-review-types-seven-families.md)). Grant and Booth's 14 types
are a subset of these ([001](../sources/001-price-2022-grant-booth-14-review-types.md)).

| Family | Named types | Defining feature |
|--------|-------------|------------------|
| Traditional | literature, narrative, critical, state-of-the-art, integrative | purposive sampling; author judgment |
| Systematic | systematic review, meta-analysis, and 10 question-specific subtypes ([003](../sources/003-munn-2018-ten-systematic-review-types.md)) | comprehensive search, appraisal, answers a question |
| Review of reviews | umbrella review, overview | includes only syntheses |
| Rapid | rapid review, rapid evidence assessment | shortcuts negotiated with a client and declared ([006](../sources/006-garritty-2021-cochrane-rapid-review-methods.md)) |
| Qualitative | qualitative evidence synthesis, meta-ethnography, thematic synthesis | aggregative or interpretive |
| Mixed methods | mixed methods review, realist review | integrates quantitative and qualitative data |
| Purpose-specific | scoping, mapping, HTA, concept analysis | tailored to one purpose |

## 3. Maps

Maps show what evidence exists without appraising it to answer a question.

- **Scoping review.** Six purposes: types of evidence, concept clarification, how research is
  conducted, key characteristics of a concept, precursor to a systematic review, knowledge gaps
  ([004](../sources/004-munn-2018-scoping-vs-systematic.md)). No quality assessment, so it cannot
  find gaps where existing research is poor ([005](../sources/005-arksey-omalley-2005-scoping-framework.md)).
- **Mapping review / systematic map.** Used interchangeably with scoping review in practice
  ([002](../sources/002-sutton-2019-48-review-types-seven-families.md)).
- **Evidence and gap map.** Systematic-review methods, output displayed as a matrix, usually
  interventions by outcomes ([007](../sources/007-white-2020-campbell-evidence-gap-maps.md)).

## 4. Argument and viewpoint pieces

From Nature's content types ([009](../sources/009-nature-2026-content-types.md)):

| Type | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Perspective | one author's forward-looking, possibly speculative view, kept balanced |
| Comment | agenda-setting expert piece proposing a solution |
| World View | call to action from personal experience |
| Correspondence, Matters Arising | responses to published work |

## 5. Decision-support products

Sutton et al. exclude these from review typologies because they name a package for a reader, not a
method ([002](../sources/002-sutton-2019-48-review-types-seven-families.md)).

- **Policy brief.** Problem, options with costs and consequences, implementation considerations,
  context-specific, built mainly from systematic reviews
  ([008](../sources/008-lavis-2009-policy-briefs.md)).
- **Evidence brief, rapid response.** Named by Sutton et al. as products; not examined here.
- **Health technology assessment.** A multi-question systematic review within an
  evidence-to-decision framework ([002](../sources/002-sutton-2019-48-review-types-seven-families.md)).

## 6. Secondary analysis

- **Analysis article.** No new data; a new analysis of existing data
  ([009](../sources/009-nature-2026-content-types.md)).

## Distinctions

- **Method against product.** "Scoping review" names a method; "policy brief" names a package. A
  product can sit on any method.
- **Map against answer.** Scoping reviews and EGMs describe the evidence; systematic reviews
  appraise it and answer a question.
- **Perspective against perspectives map.** A journal Perspective is one author's view. The
  harness's `perspectives` skill maps several parties' positions, closer to a stakeholder analysis.

## Gaps

No source retrieved here covers: horizon scanning, landscape or market analysis, stakeholder
analysis, Delphi and consensus statements, annotated bibliographies, or technical and grey-literature
reports. The typologies are health-centred; no cross-disciplinary typology was retrieved. Grant and
Booth 2009 and the full 48-type table in Sutton et al. 2019 were blocked by Cloudflare.
