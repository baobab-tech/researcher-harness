# Claims

Every factual claim and number used in `README.md` or `outputs/` appears here once. Outputs cite
claims by ID: `[C001]`. Field definitions and rules: Claims ledger in `AGENTS.md`.
`scripts/check.sh` checks required fields, source links, cited IDs, Recheck dates, and Human marks.
